<span style="font-weight: 400;">Search is changing, but SEO is not disappearing.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">For Toronto businesses, the bigger shift in 2026 is that customers no longer discover companies through one search experience.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Someone looking for a physiotherapist, renovation contractor, immigration consultant or professional service may still search Google. But that same customer can now ask ChatGPT for recommendations, use Gemini to compare providers, explore a topic through Google AI Mode or ask Perplexity to research their options.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">That means businesses are competing for [visibility in AI search results](https://ppcguru.ca/blog/ai-visibility-score-guide), AI-generated answers, citations and recommendations at the same time.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">The answer is not to abandon traditional SEO for the newest acronym.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">It is to build a strong SEO foundation and then make that foundation easier for AI-powered search systems to discover, understand and reference.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Google itself reinforced this point in May 2026, stating that established SEO best practices remain foundational for visibility in its generative AI search experiences. <strong><a href="https://developers.google.com/search">(Google Search Central)</a></strong></span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">So what actually changes with AI SEO? And what should Toronto businesses do differently?</span>
<h2><b>What Is AI SEO?</b></h2>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">AI SEO is the practice of improving a brand's visibility across traditional search engines and AI-powered discovery experiences such as Google AI Overviews, Google AI Mode, ChatGPT Search, Gemini and Perplexity.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">You may also hear related terms such as:</span>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Answer Engine Optimization (AEO)</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Generative Engine Optimization (GEO)</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">LLM optimization</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">AI search optimization</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">The terminology is still developing, and marketers do not always use these labels consistently.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">For a business owner, the terminology matters less than the goal:</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Make your business and its information easy for search engines and AI systems to discover, understand, trust and reference when potential customers research a problem you solve.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Traditional SEO primarily asked:</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">“How can this page rank higher?”</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">AI SEO adds another question:</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">“How can this information become useful enough to contribute to an AI-generated answer?”</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Those goals overlap much more than they conflict.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">The important point is that AI SEO sits on top of traditional SEO rather than replacing it.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">If Google cannot crawl your website, your pages are poorly structured, your services are unclear and your content does not satisfy real search intent, adding an “AI SEO strategy” will not solve the underlying problem.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">For businesses competing organically across Toronto and the GTA, the starting point should therefore remain a strong SEO and Local Search strategy.</span>
<h3><b>Why AI Search Matters More in 2026</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">AI-driven search is no longer an experimental corner of search behaviour.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Google reported in June 2026 that AI Overviews had more than 2.5 billion monthly active users, while AI Mode had surpassed one billion monthly users. (Google)</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Google also reported that AI Mode queries had been more than doubling each quarter since launch.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">More importantly, the way people search is changing.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Instead of typing:</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">seo company toronto</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">a user can ask:</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">“What should I look for when comparing SEO companies in Toronto for a local service business that wants both Google rankings and visibility in ChatGPT?”</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">That is a much richer query.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Google has said people use AI Mode for longer and more complex questions, and its system can use a technique called query fan-out, breaking one complex request into multiple related searches.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">This has major implications for content strategy.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">A single page optimized around one keyword is less likely to cover every question, comparison and supporting concept associated with a customer's decision.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">That makes topic clusters, internal linking and comprehensive service-page architecture increasingly valuable.</span>
<h3><b>What Traditional SEO Still Gets Right</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Businesses should be careful not to discard proven SEO work because AI search is receiving attention.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Many fundamentals remain essential.</span>
<h4><b>1. Crawlability and Indexability</b></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Search engines need to access and understand your pages.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">That includes basics such as:</span>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">sensible site architecture</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">XML sitemaps</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">appropriate robots directives</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">canonicalization</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">internal linking</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">indexable service pages</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">resolving duplicate or thin content</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">avoiding important content hidden entirely behind scripts or interfaces</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">This is still fundamental technical SEO.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">OpenAI similarly states that public websites can appear in ChatGPT Search and recommends allowing OAI-SearchBot if publishers want their content to be discoverable for ChatGPT Search. OpenAI also makes clear that inclusion or top placement cannot be guaranteed. (OpenAI Publisher FAQ)</span>
<h4><b>2. Search Intent</b></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Keywords still matter because they reveal demand.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">But strong SEO has never been about repeating keywords a specific number of times.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">A page targeting SEO Toronto, for example, should understand what someone using that query is actually trying to accomplish.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">They may want to know:</span>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">What<strong><a href="https://maps.app.goo.gl/CuaezprwSV14QVQa9"> SEO services</a> </strong>include</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">what SEO costs in Toronto</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">how long results take</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">whether they need local or national SEO</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">how agencies measure performance</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">how SEO compares with paid advertising</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">whether SEO can affect AI search visibility</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Answering those related needs creates a better page than simply using “SEO Toronto” repeatedly.</span>
<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">3. Internal Linking and Topic Clusters</span></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Suppose a Toronto SEO service page is the commercial hub.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Supporting articles could cover:</span>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Local SEO</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">technical SEO</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Google Business Profile optimization</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">AI SEO</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">SEO vs PPC</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">content clusters</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">keyword research</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">AI Overviews</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">ChatGPT Search</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">SEO measurement</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Those articles should naturally link back to the main SEO services page, while also linking between related resources.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">The result is not simply “more blog content.”</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">It creates a connected information architecture that helps users and search systems understand which pages are authoritative for each topic.</span>
<h4><b>4. Original, Useful Content</b></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">This arguably matters more now.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Google's current guidance for generative AI search specifically emphasizes valuable, unique and non-commodity content.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">If ten sites repeat the same definition of “What is GEO?”, publishing an eleventh rewrite adds little value.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">A stronger article may include:</span>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">original examples</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">first-party data</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">expert commentary</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">screenshots</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">comparisons</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">methodology</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">case observations</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Toronto-specific considerations</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">limitations and caveats</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">AI has made producing average content easier. That increases the value of information that is genuinely difficult to reproduce.</span>
<h3><b>What Changes With AI SEO?</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">The foundation stays familiar. The search surface changes.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Here are the additions businesses should make.</span>
<h4><b>1. Optimize Around Questions, Not Just Keywords</b></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">LLM users often express context that traditional keyword searches omit.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Compare:</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Traditional query:</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">“local SEO Toronto”</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">AI-style query:</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">“I own a physiotherapy clinic in North York. Should I invest in Local SEO, Google Ads or both if I want more assessment bookings?”</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">The second query contains:</span>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">industry</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">location</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">business objective</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">alternatives</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">decision criteria</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">desired outcome</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Your content strategy needs enough depth to answer those relationships.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">This is where service clusters become particularly powerful.</span>
<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">2. Write Passages That Can Stand on Their Own</span></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">AI systems frequently retrieve and synthesize specific pieces of information rather than treating every page as one indivisible document.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">That means important explanations should be clear without requiring five paragraphs of surrounding context.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">For example:</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Weak:</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">“There are several reasons this matters, as discussed above.”</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Better:</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">“Local SEO helps Toronto service businesses improve visibility for location-dependent searches by strengthening their website, Google Business Profile, local relevance and external business signals.”</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">The second statement carries meaning independently.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">That is good for readers, featured snippets and machine retrieval alike.</span>
<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">3. Make Your Business Entity Clear</span></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">An AI system should not have to guess:</span>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Who your business is</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Where you operate</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">What you sell</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Who you serve</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">What expertise you have</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Which locations belong to the company</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Which people represent the company</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Keep this information consistent across your:</span>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">website</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">About page</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">service pages</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Google Business Profile</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">professional profiles</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">reputable directories</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">relevant industry listings</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">structured data</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">For Toronto businesses, location clarity is particularly important because recommendations can depend heavily on geographic context.</span>
<h4><b>4. Strengthen Third-Party Validation</b></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">A company saying “we are experts” is a claim.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Independent evidence is stronger.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Useful external validation can include legitimate:</span>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">media coverage</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">professional associations</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">expert contributions</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">customer reviews</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">industry publications</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">business directories</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">citations</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">research references</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Recent academic work on generative search has also found meaningful differences between the sources traditional Google search retrieves and those selected by generative systems, reinforcing the importance of thinking beyond your own website.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">That does not mean manufacturing mentions or spamming forums.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">The objective should be to become genuinely reference-worthy.</span>
<h4><b>5. Use Evidence Inside Your Content</b></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">The original Generative Engine Optimization research introduced by researchers from Princeton and other institutions tested methods for improving visibility in generative responses and reported that some optimization approaches increased visibility substantially in its experimental setting. (GEO research paper)</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">The takeaway is not that adding random statistics guarantees AI citations.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">It is that well-supported content has advantages.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Whenever appropriate, include:</span>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">source names</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">dates</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">statistics</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">methodology</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">expert attribution</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">definitions</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">first-party evidence</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Most importantly, link back to the original source rather than recycling an unsourced statistic from another marketing blog.</span>
<h3><strong>Do You Need Separate AEO, GEO and SEO Strategies?</strong></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">For most Toronto businesses, probably not as three isolated programs.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">A better model is:</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Foundation: SEO</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Build:</span>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">technical health</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">crawlability</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">useful service pages</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Local SEO</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">topical authority</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">links and mentions</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">conversion tracking</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Layer 2: Answer Optimization</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Improve:</span>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">direct answers</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">FAQs where genuinely useful</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">question-based headings</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">concise definitions</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">comparison sections</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">clear entity information</span></li>
</ul>
<h4><span style="font-weight: 400;">Layer 3: Generative Search Visibility</span></h4>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">Expand:</span>
<ul>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">AI crawler accessibility</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">original evidence</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">citation-worthy resources</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">external authority</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">conversational-query coverage</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">tracking of AI mentions and referrals</span></li>
</ul>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">The same core content can support all three.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">This is far more efficient than creating one page “for Google,” another “for ChatGPT,” and another “for Gemini.”</span>
<h3><b>What Should a Toronto Business Do First?</b></h3>
<span style="font-weight: 400;">If you already invest in SEO, do not rebuild everything around AI.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Audit what you have.</span>

<span style="font-weight: 400;">Start with these seven questions:</span>
<ol>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Can search engines and AI search crawlers access our important pages?</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Does every important service have a genuinely useful page?</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Do our pages answer the questions customers ask before hiring us?</span></li>
 	<li style="font-weight: 400;" aria-level="1"><span style="font-weight: 400;">Is our business name, location, expertise and service area consistently represented?</span></li>

<h3><b>Frequently Asked Questions</b></h3>
<b>Is AI SEO replacing traditional SEO?</b><b>
</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">No. Google explicitly states that SEO best practices remain foundational for visibility in its generative AI search features. AI SEO expands traditional SEO to account for AI-generated answers, citations, conversational queries and emerging discovery platforms.</span>

<b>What is the difference between SEO and GEO?</b><b>
</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">SEO traditionally focuses on earning organic search visibility. GEO focuses on improving visibility within generative search and AI-generated answers. In practice, many useful tactics overlap, including crawlability, high-quality content, authority, clear structure and strong entity information.</span>

<b>Can SEO help my business appear in ChatGPT?</b><b>
</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">It can improve discoverability, but nobody can guarantee placement. OpenAI says public websites may appear in ChatGPT Search and recommends allowing OAI-SearchBot to crawl content intended for discovery. Ranking is determined by multiple factors related to relevance and reliability.</span>

<b>Should Toronto businesses optimize for ChatGPT, Gemini and Perplexity separately?</b><b>
</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Not initially. Start by building a technically sound, authoritative and comprehensive web presence. Once that foundation exists, monitor important queries across different platforms because sourcing behaviour can differ between AI systems.</span>

<b>Does schema markup guarantee AI citations?</b><b>
</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">No. Structured data can help search engines understand page information, but there is no supported evidence that simply adding [schema markup](https://ppcguru.ca/blog/schema-markup-for-ai-search-toronto-businesses-guide) guarantees inclusion or citations in ChatGPT, Gemini, Perplexity or Google AI results.</span>

<b>Is Local SEO still important in the AI era?</b><b>
</b><span style="font-weight: 400;">Yes. Customers still have location-specific needs, and AI tools increasingly answer local recommendation questions. Accurate business information, useful local pages, reviews, Google Business Profile optimization and strong local relevance remain important parts of an organic visibility strategy.</span>
