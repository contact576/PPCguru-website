Should a Toronto service business leave Google Ads running overnight? The answer depends on what happens after the click. An emergency plumber with a staffed night line, a clinic that accepts online bookings, and a contractor whose phone goes to voicemail at 5 p.m. need different schedules.

**Short answer:** Check the account time zone, map when your team can answer calls and process forms, then compare **qualified leads and booked jobs** by day and hour. Use the campaign ad schedule only when you want to change when the whole campaign is eligible to show. Schedule the call asset separately when the campaign can keep serving but phone calls should appear only during staffed hours. Google documents both controls in its [ad schedule setup](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6372656?hl=en) and [call asset guidance](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453991?hl=en).

## Separate campaign hours from call hours

A campaign ad schedule controls when ads are eligible to show. Google says that once you create a schedule, ads will not show outside the listed days and times ([Google Ads Help: set up an ad schedule](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6372656?hl=en)). This affects every route to that campaign's landing page, including people who would prefer to complete a form after work.

A call asset schedule controls when the phone option can appear with eligible ads. Google gives the example of showing call assets only when a business is able to take calls ([Google Ads Help: about assets](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/7331111?hl=en)). The ad can still lead to a page with a booking form outside those hours if the campaign remains eligible. Check the page itself: an always-visible phone button can still invite calls when no one answers.

| Situation | Setting to examine first | Operational check |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Staff answer calls only 9 a.m.–5 p.m.; forms work after hours | Call asset schedule | Does the form confirm receipt and set a realistic response expectation? |
| A genuine emergency service answers 24/7 | Campaign and call asset hours | Are calls actually answered overnight? |
| No calls or forms can be handled on weekends | Campaign ad schedule | Are weekend enquiries truly unusable, or can Monday follow-up book them? |
| One service has different hours from another | Separate campaign structure | Can each service have accurate ads and follow-up? |

These are decision examples, not prescriptions for every account. Review your own intake records before changing eligibility.

## Confirm the time zone before editing

Google says ad schedules use the **Google Ads account's time zone** ([Google Ads Help: set up an ad schedule](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6372656?hl=en)). A Toronto business may have an account configured in another time zone, especially if a previous agency created it. Write down the account time zone and translate your actual service hours before entering any schedule.

Also check public holidays, seasonal hours and staff coverage. A schedule that worked in summer may no longer match the winter call roster. If a campaign crosses midnight, Google's setup guide says to create schedule periods on both days rather than treating the overnight span as one day.

## Use lead quality, not clicks alone, to choose hours

The **Ad schedule** page can show performance by day, hour, or day and hour, including clicks, impressions and cost ([Google Ads Help: set up an ad schedule](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6372656?hl=en)). Those figures show when ad traffic occurred, but they do not tell you whether an enquiry was suitable or booked.

Join the time of enquiry to your CRM or appointment records. For each time block, review:

1. Spend and clicks.
2. Calls, forms and online bookings.
3. Answered versus missed calls.
4. Qualified leads.
5. Appointments or jobs booked.
6. Time from enquiry to first useful response.

For example, if late evening clicks produce online bookings that staff confirm the next morning, turning off the whole campaign at 5 p.m. could remove valuable demand. If a late evening call asset generates mostly unanswered calls, limiting that asset may be more precise. Both examples are illustrative; your own data should decide. ## Check what Google counts as a call conversion

If calls are a campaign goal, verify what the conversion action measures. Google's [call measurement guide](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6095882?hl=en) says calls from ads can be counted using a minimum duration, while later call outcomes can be imported when another system tracks them. A call that lasted past a threshold is not automatically a qualified enquiry or booked appointment.

Review call reporting and the conversion action before interpreting hourly performance. Google says call reporting uses a forwarding number to show details such as call duration and whether a call connected ([Google Ads Help: about call reporting](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2454052?hl=en)). Pair that report with your team's call disposition, where available. Avoid treating a missed call or a long wrong-number call as equal to a booked job.

## A safe first audit

Start with a read-only inventory of each campaign's current schedule and each call asset's schedule. Note the account time zone and staffing hours. Look at several weeks of day-and-hour data alongside lead outcomes, allowing for the normal delay between first enquiry and booking. Then change one clearly justified window or call asset schedule, record the change date, and review the results.

Do not copy one schedule across emergency service, consultations and planned renovations merely because all three are in Toronto. The schedule should match each service's real availability and the way its customers convert. ## Frequently asked questions

### Should Google Ads run only during business hours?

Only if the business cannot capture or use leads outside those hours. Online forms and bookings may work when the phone is unstaffed. Compare qualified leads and booked work by time before narrowing campaign hours.

### Can a call asset show only when staff answer?

Yes. Google supports scheduling call assets for specific days and hours. Check the asset's settings and confirm your actual phone coverage matches them ([Google Ads Help: about call assets](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/2453991?hl=en)).

### Which time zone does the schedule use?

The Google Ads account's time zone. Confirm it before entering local Toronto hours ([Google Ads Help: set up an ad schedule](https://support.google.com/google-ads/answer/6372656?hl=en)).
