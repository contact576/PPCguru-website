# Service-page revamp — 10 October 2026

The separate revamp branch now uses one service presentation for the existing services hub, 13 service pages and 45 service/industry pages. No routes are renamed and this is not a production release.

## Visitor journey

1. Service-specific headline, clear introduction, relevant platform artwork and a free-review action.
2. Linked partner references and an answer-first service definition.
3. Relevant evidence: original Google or Meta reports on their own service pages, a non-clickable PPC Guru website example for design/CRO, and clear reporting measures for other services.
4. Problems, agreed scope, concrete deliverables and process.
5. Detailed audit, onboarding, optimization, tool and industry information in compact native disclosures.
6. Pricing factors, reporting measures, useful location links and existing questions.
7. The existing lead form and service-specific lead routing.

Google Ads uses the 28 supplied Google reports; Meta Ads uses the 15 supplied Meta reports. The larger report reader supports keyboard navigation, dismissal, focus return and scrolling without moving the page behind it. Industry variants identify reports as wider portfolio evidence, not evidence for that particular industry. Other services do not relabel paid-ad dashboards as their own results.

All existing service definitions, service/industry angles and FAQs remain server-rendered. Scope, pricing, industry and geographic information remains accessible without requiring a carousel interaction. Metadata overrides, canonical URLs and service schema are retained. The SEO page also explains crawlable service/location pages, useful answers, consistent business facts, first-hand evidence and AI-search eligibility without promising placement.

The redesign uses the homepage's cream, green and ink palette, with compact spacing and responsive layouts. Website screenshots are examples and do not link visitors into the paid landing-page funnel. Motion and transitions respect reduced-motion preferences.

Search-performance evidence and preservation priorities are recorded in `SEO-REVAMP-PRESERVATION.md`. New dates apply to the materially revised homepage and service routes; other page families retain their existing revision dates.
